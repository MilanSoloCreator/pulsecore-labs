Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

PulseCore Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to transparency regarding our applications and services distributed through platforms including the Google Play Store.

1. Information Collection & Use

Our mobile applications and core utilities are engineered to operate primarily on-device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. When diagnostics or basic telemetry are required to maintain application stability, we utilize anonymized crash reporting with explicit platform-level consent.

2. Device Permissions

Any runtime permission requested by our applications is strictly necessary for core functionality (e.g., local storage access to save progress or settings). Permissions are never leveraged for advertising or third-party behavioral profiling.

3. Compliance with Play Console Policies

We adhere fully to Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy, Families Policy Requirements (where applicable), and Target API level standards.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions or inquiries regarding your data or our policies, please contact our privacy compliance team at:
